Privacy Policy

Brain Playground · Last updated: 3 October 2026

Short version: Brain Playground has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. We do not ask for or collect personal information from children or parents. Progress stays on your device.

Who we are

Brain Playground is a children's brain-games app made by Muhammed Baqir (Kerala, India). Contact: YOUR_EMAIL.

What stays on the device

  • The child's first name (or nickname) typed on first open.
  • Game progress: levels, stars, streaks, badges, settings.
  • The parent PIN and recovery answer, stored in a scrambled (hashed) form.

This information never leaves the device. We cannot see it. Uninstalling the app or using "Reset progress" in the Parent corner deletes it. Please use a nickname rather than a full name.

What the app downloads

To show new games, questions, notices and sponsor cards, the app downloads a content file from our cloud service (Supabase). The app sends no personal information with this request. Like any internet request, the service can see the device's IP address and technical details such as app version. We use this only to deliver the content and keep the service secure. The app also loads a font (Google Fonts) and a software library (jsDelivr) over the internet, which see the same kind of technical request data.

Sponsors and links

Some screens show a "Sponsored" card from a partner. These cards are chosen by us, not by an ad network. They do not use advertising IDs, do not track children and are not personalised. A sponsor link opens only after a parent enters the Parent PIN, and then opens in the phone's web browser. That website has its own privacy policy.

Children

The app is made for children and follows the principle of collecting nothing about them. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. We do not sell or share data, and the app has no chat, social features, purchases or location access.

Parent corner

The Parent corner shows progress and settings. It is protected by a PIN that the parent chooses. If a parent forgets the PIN, the recovery question resets it.

Security

Content is delivered over HTTPS. Only authorised administrators can change the content. Administrator accounts are separate from the app.

Your choices

Because we hold no personal data about you or your child, there is nothing for us to access or delete. To remove everything on the device, use "Reset progress" or uninstall the app. For questions or requests, write to YOUR_EMAIL.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, show a notice inside the app.
